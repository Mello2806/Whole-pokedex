# 🎒 Pokémon GO Tracker (prototype)

A fan-made app to keep track of your Pokémon GO progress. It is a single page (`index.html`): no sign-up, no server, no ads.

- **💎 Sinnoh Stone, ✨ Special evolutions, 📚 All the others:** Candy, copies and priorities for every evolution. Candy is shared by the whole family: update it on one card and the whole line follows.
- **🗂️ Living Dex:** which Pokémon you keep in storage, by region, with hints about what you can get by evolving something you already have.
- **📖 Pokédex:** registered, ✨ shiny, 🍀 lucky and ⭐ 3 stars, with types and stats.
- **🦋 Vivillon:** the 18 regional patterns for the medal plus the event ones.
- **🎯 Hunt list:** mark what you are looking for and filter by it.

## How to use

Open `index.html` in your browser. Your progress is saved **only in your browser**, and nothing is sent anywhere.
Use **💾 Backup** now and then to download a copy, and **📂 Restore** to bring it back or move it to another device.
Clearing your browser data erases any progress that is not in a backup.
